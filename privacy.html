Privacy Policy & Ethical Promise

Last updated: August 4, 2026

1. Introduction

At ROSEVERSE Atelier, accessible from roseverse-website.vercel.app, your privacy, trust, and security are our highest priorities. We operate under strict ethical communication standards, ensuring no false claims, no silent tracking, and zero selling of user data to third parties.

2. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when booking a 1-on-1 consultation or subscribing to our newsletter, including your name, professional email address, project requirements, and preferred meeting dates.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to fulfill your consultation requests, manage commercial licensing agreements, provide technical support for our 300-frame canvas engine, and send seasonal botanical notes if subscribed.

4. Data Protection & Security

We implement enterprise-grade security controls, HTTPS transport encryption, Content Security Policies (CSP), and secure input validation to protect your data against unauthorized access or disclosure.

5. Contact Us

If you have questions regarding our privacy practices or wish to request data deletion, contact our ethical compliance team at privacy@roseverse.com.
